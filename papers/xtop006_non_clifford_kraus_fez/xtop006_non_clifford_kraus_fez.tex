\section{Introduction}
Distinguishing genuine unitary operator growth from Markovian or non-Markovian decoherence is a central methodological requirement of quantum information scrambling experiments~\cite{YoshidaKitaev2017,LandsmanFiggatt2019}. Standard out-of-time-order correlators decay both when local Pauli operators spread into high-weight strings and when single-qubit or two-qubit noise damps expectation values. Verified teleportation via the Yoshida--Kitaev decoding protocol resolves this ambiguity in principle by converting operator spreading into a positive recovery shift on Bob's output wire conditioned on projecting a single Einstein--Podolsky--Rosen pair onto $|\Phi^+\rangle$.

Two experimental confounds nevertheless arise when sweeping a continuous non-Clifford scrambling family $U(\theta)$ on superconducting processors: (i) standard transpilers collapse special Clifford angles ($\theta = 0, \pi/2$) to shorter two-qubit gate counts than generic non-Clifford angles ($\theta = \pi/8, \pi/4, 3\pi/8$), coupling scrambling angle to $T_1/T_2$ idle decay, and (ii) un-stratified twirl controls that assign distinct Pauli operators across temporal blocks mix deterministic twirl-to-twirl dispersion into block standard errors. Here we eliminate both confounds on \texttt{ibm\_fez}.

\section{Analytical Kraus Trajectory and Constant-Skeleton Compilation}
Consider the seven-qubit register $(q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3, q_4, q_5, q_6)$ initialized with Alice's input $\rho_{\mathrm{in}}$ on $q_0$ and three Bell pairs on $(q_1, q_5)$, $(q_2, q_4)$, and $(q_3, q_6)$. Applying $U(\theta)$ to $(q_0, q_1, q_2)$ and $U^*(\theta) = U(-\theta)$ to $(q_3, q_5, q_4)$ followed by $|\Phi^+\rangle$ projection on $(q_2, q_4)$ induces sixteen $2\times 2$ remnant Kraus operators
\begin{equation}
  (M_r(\theta))_{xa} = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{b,c,k \in \{0,1\}} U_{ijk,abc}(\theta)\,U^*_{nmk,xbc}(\theta),
\end{equation}
indexed by the four unmeasured remnant wires $r = (i,j,m,n)$ with output wire $x = q_6$. Defining the tunable two-qubit entangler $G_{c,t}(\theta) = e^{i\theta/2} R_z(\theta)_c R_x(\theta)_t \,\mathrm{CZ}_{c,t}\, R_x(-\theta)_t \,\mathrm{CZ}_{c,t}$ and setting $U(\theta) = G_{0,2}(\theta)G_{0,1}(\theta)H^{\otimes 3}G_{0,2}(\theta)G_{0,1}(\theta)H^{\otimes 3}$ yields the exact closed-form trajectory
\begin{equation}
  P_{\Phi^+}(\theta) = \left(1 - \tfrac{1}{2}\sin^2\theta\right)^2, \qquad
  F_6(\theta \mid \Phi^+) = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6 P_{\Phi^+}(\theta)},
\end{equation}
saturating the unitary branching invariant $P_{\Phi^+}(\theta)F_{\mathrm{ent}}(\theta \mid \Phi^+) = 1/4$ identically across $\theta \in [0, \pi/2]$ while reaching non-Clifford stabilizer R\'enyi magic $M_2 = 4$ bits at $\theta = \pi/4$.
