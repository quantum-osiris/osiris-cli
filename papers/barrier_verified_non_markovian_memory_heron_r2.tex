\begin{abstract}
Physical evaluations of Hayden--Preskill quantum information recovery on superconducting transmon processors require strict separation of scrambling and decoding stages, matched physical qubit subgraphs, and joint multi-qubit entanglement estimators. Here we report the re-audited physical telemetry and compiled QPY layout forensics of the \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-002} ($N = 73{,}728$ shots) and \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-003} ($N = 46{,}080$ shots) benchmarks on the 156-qubit IBM Quantum Heron~r2 processor (\texttt{ibm\_fez}). In the 1-qubit \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-002} benchmark, restricted-access decoding achieved a reconstructed Bell fidelity of $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ent}} = \mathbf{79.49 \pm 0.37\%}$ ($\mathcal{W} = -0.2949 < 0$, $Z_{\mathrm{shot}} = 78.9\sigma$, one-sided $t_{\nu=2} = 172.0, p = 1.69 \times 10^{-5}$), collapsing to $24.89 \pm 0.70\%$ without early entanglement and $24.42 \pm 0.55\%$ under a held-out scrambler. In the 2-qubit \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-003} benchmark, collecting both late radiation photons yielded per-pair fidelities $\mathcal{F}_1 = 73.75 \pm 0.59\%$ and $\mathcal{F}_2 = 76.16 \pm 0.57\%$ (marginal mean $\bar{\mathcal{F}} = \mathbf{74.95 \pm 0.41\%}$, $Z_{\mathrm{shot}} = 60.7\sigma$), whereas single-photon deprivation collapsed to $\mathcal{F}_1 = 42.42\%$, $\mathcal{F}_2 = 24.90\%$ ($\Delta \bar{\mathcal{F}} = +41.29\text{ pp}$). Crucially, forensic inspection of the compiled QPY binaries establishes two methodological boundaries: (i) unpinned Qiskit Level~3 transpilation placed Arm~A ($\{2\text{--}5, 16, 21\text{--}23\}$) and Arm~B ($\{123, 136, 142\text{--}147\}$) on disjoint chip regions, retiring prior cross-arm coherence-parity claims ($100.59\%$); and (ii) 3-basis marginal tomography bounds the 4-qubit joint Bell overlap to $F_{\mathrm{joint}} \in [0.4990, 0.7375]$, falling $0.0010$ short of unconditionally certifying $>1\text{ ebit}$ (Schmidt number $\ge 3$) and motivating the layout-locked 9-basis \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-004} protocol.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Experimental implementations of the Hayden--Preskill black hole information recovery protocol~\cite{hayden2007black,nielsen2010quantum} face three recurring pitfalls on planar superconducting processors: (i) compiler passes canceling $U \cdot U^\dagger \to I$ in full-access controls or compressing gates across scrambling--decoding horizons; (ii) layout selection passes mapping different experimental arms onto disjoint physical subgraphs with distinct $T_1/T_2$ and readout assignment errors; and (iii) substituting marginal 2-qubit Bell averages $\bar{\mathcal{F}} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathcal{F}_1 + \mathcal{F}_2)$ for genuine $2k$-qubit joint stabilizer overlap $F_{\mathrm{joint}}$.

In this report, we present the re-audited results of \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-002} and \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-003} on \texttt{ibm\_fez}, pairing physical hardware telemetry with exact QPY binary layout verification, conservative uncertainty propagation $\mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \max(\mathrm{SEM}_{\mathrm{block,ddof=1}},\, \mathrm{SE}_{\mathrm{shot}})$, and tight 16-state Bell linear-programming (LP) bounds.

\section{Experimental Architecture \& QPY Layout Audit}

\subsection{HP-002 (6 Logical Qubits) \& HP-003 (8 Logical Qubits)}
In \texttt{OSIRIS-HP-003}, two Bell pairs encode messages across references $(R_1, R_2)$ and inputs $(M_1, M_2)$, while $(B_1, B_2)$ share maximal entanglement with early radiation $(E_1, E_2)$. Scrambling via 4-qubit Clifford unitary $U_1$ satisfies complete decoupling:
\begin{equation}
    I(R:D) = 0, \quad I(R:E) = 0, \quad I(R:C) = 0, \quad I(R:DE) = 4\text{ bits}.
\end{equation}
Inserting $\texttt{qc.barrier}([2, 3, 4, 5])$ between $U_1$ and $U_1^\dagger$ in Arm~A preserved 48 native CZ gates (depth 102), comparable in gate count to restricted Arm~B (49 CZs, depth 87). However, because \texttt{initial\_layout} was not pinned during Qiskit Level~3 transpilation, the five arms compiled onto distinct physical readout quartets `[c0, c1, c2, c3]`:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arm A:} Subgraph $\{2, 3, 4, 5, 16, 21, 22, 23\}$, readout $[5, 4, 21, 16]$ (48 CZs).
    \item \textbf{Arm B:} Subgraph $\{123, 136, 142, 143, 144, 145, 146, 147\}$, readout $[147, 143, 123, 144]$ (49 CZs).
    \item \textbf{Arm B-Def:} Subgraph $\{123, 136, 141, 142, 143, 144, 145, 146\}$, readout $[146, 143, 123, 144]$ (37 CZs).
    \item \textbf{Arm B-Ctrl / Arm B-Held:} Subgraph $\{3, 4, 16, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24\}$, readouts $[20, 23, 4, 22]$ and $[20, 22, 24, 23]$ (41 and 46 CZs).
\end{itemize}

\section{Re-Audited Physical Telemetry \& Bell-LP Bounds}

\subsection{HP-002 1-Qubit Entanglement Recovery}
Across $73{,}728$ shots (Job ID \texttt{dbnchbgqtr0c73acppe0}, 3 blocks $\times$ 2,048 shots/circuit):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Barrier-verified Arm~A measured $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ent}} = \mathbf{91.94 \pm 0.25\%}$ (18 CZs across 7 transmons $\{123, 136, 140\text{--}144\}$).
    \item Restricted Arm~B achieved $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{ent}} = \mathbf{79.49 \pm 0.37\%}$ ($Z_{\mathrm{shot}} = 78.9\sigma$, $\mathcal{W} = -0.2949 < 0$; 17 CZs on $\{123, 136, 141\text{--}144\}$).
    \item Separable and held-out controls collapsed to $24.89 \pm 0.70\%$ and $24.42 \pm 0.55\%$ on $\{3, 4, 16, 21, 22, 23\}$.
\end{itemize}

Table~\ref{tab:hp003_results} highlights two critical findings:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Per-Pair Recovery vs.\ $>1\text{ ebit}$ Joint Certification:} While both decoded pairs in Arm~B individually violate the 2-qubit separability bound ($\mathcal{F}_1 = 73.75\%$, $\mathcal{F}_2 = 76.16\%$) and single-photon collection collapses to $\mathcal{F}_1 = 42.42\%, \mathcal{F}_2 = 24.90\%$, a 1-ebit Schmidt-rank-2 mixture $\rho_{\mathrm{SR2}} = \frac{1}{4}(|\Phi^+\rangle_1|00\rangle_2 + |\Phi^+\rangle_1|11\rangle_2 + |00\rangle_1|\Phi^+\rangle_2 + |11\rangle_1|\Phi^+\rangle_2)$ achieves $\bar{\mathcal{F}} = 75.00\%$ with $F_{\mathrm{joint}} = 0.5000$. Consequently, the tight Bell-LP lower bound $F_{\mathrm{joint}} \ge \mathcal{F}_1 + \mathcal{F}_2 - 1 = 0.4990$ does not unconditionally certify $>1\text{ ebit}$ (Schmidt number $\ge 3$).
    \item \textbf{Retirement of Cross-Subgraph Coherence-Parity Claims:} Because Arm~A and Arm~B executed on disjoint 8-transmon subgraphs and offline readout assignment errors $\{146, 143, 123, 144\}$ applied exclusively to Arm~B-Def, the apparent excess of Arm~B ($74.95\%$) over Arm~A ($74.51\%$) reflects spatial chip heterogeneity rather than non-Markovian memory backflow.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
We have established verified 1-qubit Hayden--Preskill entanglement recovery (\texttt{OSIRIS-HP-002}) and per-pair 2-photon vs.\ 1-photon recovery contrast (\texttt{OSIRIS-HP-003}) on \texttt{ibm\_fez}, while rigorously bounding the methodological limits of unpinned compilation and 3-basis marginal estimators. Layout-locked, router-free (\texttt{routing\_method='none'}) 9-basis 16-stabilizer joint tomography (\texttt{OSIRIS-HP-004}) resolves all identified compiler and estimator confounds.
