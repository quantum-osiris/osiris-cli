\begin{abstract}
Digital quantum simulation of continuous $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Yang--Mills lattice gauge theories via discrete non-abelian subgroups requires simultaneous control of local Gauss-law gauge invariance, Peter--Weyl electric Casimir spectra, and multi-link holonomy gate synthesis. Building on our verified 5-qubit semidirect encoding \texttt{bt2t-enc-1} ($2T = Q_8 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_3 \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$, $|2T|=\BtpGroupOrder$, SHA-256 prefix \texttt{\BtpEncShaPrefix\ldots}), we formulate and exhaustively verify (\texttt{SIMULATED}, \texttt{provider\_job\_id: null}, 0\,s QPU, \BtpChecksTotal/\BtpChecksTotal{} checks passed) the Kogut--Susskind single-plaquette Hamiltonian $H_{\mathrm{KS}} = \frac{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}{2} H_E + \frac{1}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2} H_B$. First, we construct the bi-invariant degree-\BtpCayleyDeg{} 24-cell Cayley electric Laplacian $\Delta_{2T}$ (diameter~\BtpCayleyDiam{}), proving exact left/right translation invariance and machine-precision diagonalization ($\|\mathcal{F}_{2T}\Delta_{\mathrm{SU}(2)}\mathcal{F}_{2T}^\dagger - D_{\mathrm{Cas}}\|_2 = \BtpPwDiagErr$) into calibrated $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Casimir eigenvalues $\{0, \BtpCasRhoTwoZero, \BtpCasRhoThree, \BtpCasRhoTwoOne, \BtpCasChiOne\}$ across all \BtpConjClasses{} irreducible representations while decoupling all 8 unused basis codewords with \BtpUnusedLeak{} leakage. Second, verifying all \BtpTwoLinkConfigs{} two-link (15-qubit) and \BtpFourLinkConfigs{} four-link (25-qubit) group configurations, we establish an exact 4-vertex Gauss-law intertwining isomorphism onto a single 5-qubit link register spanning the \BtpGaussDim-dimensional class-function subspace (maximum $L_2$ state discrepancy $\BtpFourLinkRandErr$ across arbitrary gauge-invariant superpositions, yielding a $\BtpQubitRedTwenty\times$--$\BtpQubitRedTwentyFive\times$ qubit compression). Third, structured Walsh--Gray synthesis compiles the magnetic step $U_B(\Delta t)$ in \BtpCzUbAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ} (\BtpCzUbHh{} heavy-hex $T_5$ \texttt{CZ}) and a complete second-order Strang--Trotter step (empirical log-log error slope \BtpStrangSlopeGOne{}, reaching $\|\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{Strang}}^N - e^{-i H_{\mathrm{KS}} T}\|_2 = \BtpFineStrangErr$ at $N=2{,}048$) in \BtpCzStrangAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ} (\BtpCzStrangHh{} heavy-hex $T_5$ \texttt{CZ}, \BtpZzStrangHtwo{} Quantinuum \texttt{H2} \texttt{ZZPhase})—a $\BtpGainDenseAa\times$ compression over dense 5-qubit synthesis (\BtpCzDenseAa{} \texttt{CZ}) and a $\BtpGainTwentyAa\times$--$\BtpGainTwentyFiveHh\times$ compression over unfixed 20- and 25-qubit four-link circuits (\BtpCzTwentyAa{}--\BtpCzTwentyFiveHh{} \texttt{CZ}).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Hamiltonian lattice gauge theory~\cite{kogut1975,zohar2016} discretizes non-abelian Yang--Mills fields onto oriented lattice links carrying group-valued parallel transporters $U_\ell \in G \subset \mathrm{SU}(N_c)$ coupled at vertices by local Gauss-law constraints. Approximating continuous $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ link variables by crystal-like finite non-abelian subgroups~\cite{lang1982,lamm2019,alam2022} eliminates infinite-dimensional bosonic truncation error while preserving exact non-commutative gauge transformations. Within the ADE classification of finite subgroups of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, the binary tetrahedral group $2T \cong \mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{F}_3)$ of order $|2T| = \BtpGroupOrder$ (whose elements form the vertices of the regular four-dimensional 24-cell) is the smallest subgroup possessing genuine tetrahedral rotational symmetry and three-dimensional irreducible representations.

In Milestones~1 and~2 of this series, we verified exact 5-qubit $2T$ inversion on live \texttt{ibm\_fez} hardware (\texttt{OSIRIS-BT2T-001}~\cite{davis_bt2t001}) and constructed semidirect 10-qubit reversible multiplication $U_{\mathrm{mul}}|g\rangle|h\rangle = |g\rangle|gh\rangle$ alongside the 5-qubit non-abelian Peter--Weyl quantum Fourier transform $\mathcal{F}_{2T}$ (\texttt{OSIRIS-BT2T-002}~\cite{davis_bt2t002}). Here, in Milestone~3 (\texttt{OSIRIS-BT2T-003}, strictly \texttt{SIMULATED} and offline \texttt{PROXY}-compiled, \texttt{provider\_job\_id: null}, 0\,s QPU), we assemble those group primitives into a complete Kogut--Susskind single-plaquette Trotter simulation, proving exact 4-vertex Gauss-law reduction from 20--25 qubits to 5 qubits and quantifying native \texttt{CZ} and \texttt{ZZPhase} gate counts across all-to-all QCCD and heavy-hex superconducting topologies.

On the 32-dimensional 5-qubit Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_5 = (\mathbb{C}^2)^{\otimes 5}$, encoding \texttt{bt2t-enc-1} maps $2T = Q_8 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_3$ onto computational basis states $\{|0\rangle, \ldots, |23\rangle\}$ while isolating $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{unused}} = \mathrm{span}\{|24\rangle, \ldots, |31\rangle\}$. The nearest-neighbor shell around $\mathbf{1} \in 2T$ on the unit 24-cell in $S^3 \subset \mathbb{H}$ is the \BtpCayleyDeg-element tetrahedralconjugacy union $S_8 = \{\frac{1}{2}(+\mathbf{1} \pm \mathbf{i} \pm \mathbf{j} \pm \mathbf{k})\}$, which is inverse-closed ($S_8 = S_8^{-1}$), normal ($g S_8 g^{-1} = S_8$ for all $g \in 2T$), and generates $2T$ with graph diameter~\BtpCayleyDiam{}.

Because $S_8$ is a union of complete conjugacy classes, its Cayley graph Laplacian $\Delta_{2T} = 8 I_{24} - \sum_{s \in S_8} L(s)$ is strictly bi-invariant ($[\Delta_{2T}, L(g)] = [\Delta_{2T}, R(g)] = 0$) and acts by Schur's lemma as a scalar multiple on each Plancherel block $d_\lambda \times d_\lambda$ under $\mathcal{F}_{2T}$. Scaling $\Delta_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} = \frac{3}{16}\Delta_{2T}$ matches the continuum $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ quadratic Casimir $j(j+1) = \BtpCasRhoTwoZero$ on the faithful spin-$1/2$ doublet $\rho_{2,0}$ and $2\cdot\frac{3}{4} = \BtpCasRhoThree$ on the spin-$1$ triplet $\rho_3$ (Table~\ref{tab:btp_irreps}). Pairing $H_E = \Delta_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} \oplus 0_8$ with the fundamental Wilson-loop magnetic operator $H_B = -\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{Tr}_{\rho_{2,0}}(U_{\Box}) \oplus 0_8$ (where $\mathrm{Tr}_{\rho_{2,0}}(g) \in \{-2, -1, 0, +1, +2\}$) defines the Kogut--Susskind plaquette Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
H_{\mathrm{KS}}(g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2) = \frac{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}{2}\, H_E + \frac{1}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}\, H_B,
\label{eq:h_ks}
\end{equation}
which commutes identically with adjoint gauge transformations $C(h) = L(h)R(h^{-1})$ for all $h \in 2T$ and acts as the exact zero generator ($U = I_8$, leakage \BtpUnusedLeak{}) on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{unused}}$.

An unfixed elementary plaquette carries four link registers $|u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_{20}$ with oriented holonomy $u_{\Box} = u_1 u_2 u_3^{-1} u_4^{-1} \in 2T$ and four vertex Gauss-law operators
\begin{align}
G_0(h)|u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4\rangle &= |h u_1, u_2, u_3, h u_4\rangle, \nonumber\\
G_1(h)|u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4\rangle &= |u_1 h^{-1}, h u_2, u_3, u_4\rangle,
\label{eq:gauss_vertices}
\end{align}
together with analogous vertex actions $G_2(h), G_3(h)$. Exhaustively testing our reversible multi-link holonomy circuits across all $24^3 = \BtpTwoLinkConfigs$ two-link configurations (15 qubits) and all $24^4 = \BtpFourLinkConfigs$ four-link configurations (25 qubits with clean ancilla uncomputation) verified zero basis or phase errors.

Fixing three links along a maximal spanning tree of the 4-vertex loop ($u_2 = u_3 = u_4 = \mathbf{1}$) defines an exact isometric embedding $\mathcal{J}: \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{inv}}^{(5\mathrm{Q})} \to \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{inv}}^{(20\mathrm{Q})}$ on the \BtpGaussDim-dimensional space of conjugation-invariant class functions ($\dim \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{inv}} = \BtpConjClasses$, intertwining link Laplacians to $\BtpIntertwineErr$). Across five multi-link Strang steps, 20-qubit four-link evolution and 5-qubit gauge-fixed evolution agree to $\BtpFourLinkBasisErr$ on all \BtpGaussDim{} class-basis states and $\BtpFourLinkRandErr$ on random gauge-invariant superpositions ($\BtpTwoLinkErr$ for two-link reduction), preserving residual conjugation invariance $C(h)|\psi(t)\rangle = |\psi(t)\rangle$ to $\BtpGaussDriftErr$.

Across $g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2 \in \{0.5, 1.0, 2.0\}$ and $N \in \{8, 16, \ldots, 2{,}048\}$ steps (Fig.~\ref{fig:btp_trotter} and Table~\ref{tab:btp_regimes}), first-order Lie--Trotter slopes lie in $[\BtpMinLieSlope, \BtpMaxLieSlope]$ while second-order Strang slopes lie in $[\BtpMinStrangSlope, \BtpMaxStrangSlope]$. Starting from $|\chi_0\rangle$, strong coupling ($g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2=2$) confines population near the electric ground state ($P(\chi_0)$ dominant), whereas weak coupling ($g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2=0.5$) excites higher representations ($\rho_{2,0}, \rho_{2,1}, \rho_3$) through coherent magnetic plaquette transitions.

Exploiting the semidirect bit layout $|c_1, c_0, s, a, b\rangle$ of \texttt{bt2t-enc-1}, the magnetic propagator $U_B(\Delta t) = \exp(-i\frac{\Delta t}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2} H_B)$ is diagonal in the computational basis and depends only on $(c_1, c_0, s)$ and $(a \lor b)$, admitting a sparse Walsh--Gray phase-polynomial decomposition in \BtpCzUbAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ} gates (\BtpCzUbHh{} \texttt{CZ} on a 5-qubit heavy-hex $T_5$ tree; unitary error $\BtpUbDirectErr$). Similarly, because $H_E$ commutes with $Q_8$-index permutations and respects the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ coset structure, structured synthesis realizes $U_E(\Delta t)$ in \BtpCzUeAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ} (\BtpCzUeHh{} heavy-hex \texttt{CZ}; error $\BtpUeDirectErr$) without incurring the twice-applied QFT cost of $\mathcal{F}_{2T}^\dagger e^{-i \frac{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2 \Delta t}{2} D_{\mathrm{Cas}}} \mathcal{F}_{2T}$ (\BtpCzQsdAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ}).

Combining $U_B(\Delta t/2) U_E(\Delta t) U_B(\Delta t/2)$ yields a complete second-order Strang step in \BtpCzStrangAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ}, \BtpCzStrangHh{} heavy-hex $T_5$ \texttt{CZ}, or \BtpZzStrangHtwo{} Quantinuum \texttt{H2} \texttt{ZZPhase} gates (direct circuit error $\BtpStrangDirectErr$; Table~\ref{tab:btp_synthesis} and Fig.~\ref{fig:btp_cz}). Relative to generic 5-qubit Shannon decomposition (\BtpCzDenseAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ}, \BtpCzDenseHh{} heavy-hex \texttt{CZ}, \BtpZzDenseHtwo{} \texttt{ZZPhase}), structured synthesis saves $\BtpGainDenseAa\times$ ($\BtpGainDenseHh\times$ on heavy-hex). Relative to unfixed 4-link plaquette circuits requiring sequential 10-qubit $2T$ multiplications and inversions (\BtpCzTwentyAa{} and \BtpCzTwentyFiveAa{} all-to-all \texttt{CZ}; \BtpCzTwentyHh{} and \BtpCzTwentyFiveHh{} heavy-hex \texttt{CZ}), maximal-tree gauge fixing plus structured 5-qubit synthesis achieves $\BtpGainTwentyAa\times$ to $\BtpGainTwentyFiveHh\times$ native two-qubit gate compression—bringing multi-step non-abelian $2T$ plaquette dynamics well within Quantinuum \texttt{H2-1} coherence budgets.

\section{Conclusion and Data Availability}\label{sec:data}

Maximal-tree Gauss-law reduction combined with semidirect Walsh--Gray synthesis compresses single-plaquette Kogut--Susskind $2T \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$ dynamics from tens of thousands of two-qubit gates on 20--25 qubits down to \BtpCzStrangAa{} \texttt{CZ} (\BtpZzStrangHtwo{} \texttt{ZZPhase}) per Strang step on 5 qubits with machine-precision gauge equivalence. Scope note: all results in \texttt{OSIRIS-BT2T-003} are exact \texttt{SIMULATED} statevector/operator calculations and local \texttt{PROXY} compilations (0\,s QPU); coupled multi-plaquette ladders where non-local magnetic terms remain dynamically coupled across shared links form the natural next milestone.
